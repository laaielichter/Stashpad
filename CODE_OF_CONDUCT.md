# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, give and take feedback on the work rather than the person, and do not harass, insult or share anyone's private information.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/laaielichter/stashpad/security/advisories/new) (the only private channel on this repository); reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules and may block people who keep breaking them.
