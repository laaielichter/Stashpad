# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Stashpad to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their background, experience or identity. We pledge to make taking part in this project free of harassment.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, or comments that put down a group of people.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, [@laaielichter](https://github.com/laaielichter), on GitHub. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code.
